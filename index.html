Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about repairs, removals, and maintenance across Hamilton & the GTA.

Do you repair elliptical machines in Hamilton?

Yes! We provide same-day elliptical repairs across Hamilton, including stride mechanism fixes, resistance calibration, and console replacements for all major brands.

Can you fix exercise bikes at home?

Absolutely. Our mobile technician come to your home or gym to repair exercise bikes - from pedal replacements to resistance system overhauls - with a 30-day warranty on all parts.

How quickly can you repair a rowing machine?

Most rower repairs are completed in 60-90 minutes during our first visit. We carry common parts like rowing belts, chains, and sensors for immediate fixes.

What strength equipment do you service?

We repair all cable-based strength machines including functional trainers, selectorized equipment, plate-loaded machines, and power racks. Common fixes include cable replacements, pulley repairs, and pad restorations.

Do you remove old treadmills in Hamilton?

Yes! We provide professional treadmill removal services across Hamilton and GTA. We disassemble, remove and recycle your old treadmill with eco-friendly disposal methods.

Do you offer commercial gym maintenance contracts?

Yes, we provide customized maintenance plans for fitness facilities including quarterly tune-ups, emergency repair priority, and discounted parts for contract holders.
