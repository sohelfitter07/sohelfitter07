Common LifeFitness Problems We Fix

Treadmill Issues

  • Belt slipping or misalignment
  • Incline/decline mechanism failure
  • Console display malfunctions
  • Motor controller problems
  • Error codes (E1, E3, E6)
  • Heart rate monitor failures

Elliptical & Bike Problems

  • Stride mechanism issues
  • Resistance control failures
  • Squeaking or grinding noises
  • Power supply problems
  • Pedal and crank assembly repairs
  • Heart rate sensor malfunctions

Commercial Equipment

  • LifeFitness Platinum Club maintenance
  • Touchscreen console repairs
  • Drive system replacements
  • Deck replacement and lubrication
  • Electronic calibration services
  • Preventative maintenance programs
