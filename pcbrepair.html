Common PCB faults

Circuit Board Failures We Repair

These are the board faults we see most often in treadmills, ellipticals and other cardio equipment.

Power supply failure

No power or intermittent shutdowns caused by blown components or faulty low-voltage circuits.

Burnt MOSFET or IGBT

Board overheating, sparks or a machine that stops suddenly under load.

Relay and fuse issues

The machine clicks but the motor does not respond, or it will not start at all.

Motor drive circuit errors

Error codes such as E1 or E2, or a belt that does not move when commanded.

Console communication loss

The display works but does not send commands to the motor board.

Sensor and solder faults

Over-temperature shutdowns, loose connectors and cracked solder joints causing intermittent faults.

Our process

PCB Repair Process

A systematic approach that fixes the cause, not just the symptom.

1

Initial diagnostic

Visual inspection and basic electrical testing to identify burnt components, bulging capacitors or damaged traces.

2

Component-level testing

MOSFETs, capacitors, resistors, ICs and connectors are tested individually to find failed parts.

3

Circuit analysis

We trace how the components interact to understand why the part failed in the first place.

4

Repair & replacement

Failed components are replaced with quality parts using proper soldering and thermal management.

5

Testing & prevention

The board is tested under load, and upgrades are applied where they reduce repeat failures.

Questions & answers

PCB Repair FAQs

What can be repaired, how long it takes and when replacement makes more sense.

Can every board be repaired?

No, but many can. We diagnose first and tell you honestly whether component-level repair is viable. If the board is too damaged or parts are unavailable, we quote a replacement instead.

How long does a PCB repair take?

Many common board faults are repaired within a few business days. Turnaround depends on the fault and parts availability, and we confirm it after diagnosis.

Do you repair the board on-site or at a bench?

Some faults can be diagnosed on-site. Board-level repair is usually completed at our bench, where we have the right soldering and testing equipment.

What brands of boards do you work on?

We repair boards from Life Fitness, NordicTrack, Precor, Matrix, Bowflex, Technogym, ProForm, Smooth Fitness and most other major fitness brands.

Is a repaired board reliable?

We replace failed components with OEM or higher-rated parts, address thermal issues where relevant and load-test the board before it goes back into service. Repairs carry a 30-day warranty.

Is repair cheaper than a new board?

Usually, yes. Component-level repair typically costs a fraction of a replacement board, and far less than a new machine. You receive a written quote before any repair begins.
