Common symptoms

Signs Your Equipment Needs Attention

These are the complaints we hear most often. Catching them early usually means a smaller, cheaper repair.

Noisy operation

Grinding, squeaking or knocking usually points to bearings, rollers, motor brushes or a dry deck.

Belt slipping or tracking

A loose or worn belt can slip during a workout and become a safety risk.

Incline or resistance issues

The machine moves unevenly, will not change resistance or makes a clicking sound.

Console or display faults

Blank screens, unresponsive buttons and error codes often trace to electronics or wiring.

Loose or wobbly frame

Loose bolts, worn bushings and damaged stabilizers affect safety and alignment.

Sluggish performance

The machine feels harder to use, speed surges or it shuts down mid-workout.

How a tune-up works

Our 5-Step Tune-Up

A tune-up is more than a wipe-down. We inspect, lubricate, calibrate, tighten and test.

1

Inspect

We check belts, decks, bearings, cables, electronics and safety hardware for wear.

2

Lubricate

We apply the right lubricant to the right surfaces without over-oiling.

3

Calibrate

Resistance, incline and electronic systems are adjusted to manufacturer specifications.

4

Tighten & clean

Bolts, fasteners and connections are secured, and the machine is cleaned.

5

Test & hand over

We test under load, explain what was done and note anything to watch.

Questions & answers

Repair & Tune-Up FAQs

What is included, how long it takes and when a tune-up is enough.

What is included in a tune-up?

A tune-up includes inspection, lubrication, calibration, hardware tightening, cleaning and a performance test. We note any worn parts that should be watched or replaced.

How often should I tune up my equipment?

Home equipment usually benefits from an annual tune-up. Commercial machines with heavy daily use often need quarterly or monthly service depending on traffic.

Is a tune-up enough if the machine is broken?

No. A tune-up maintains a working machine. If a part has failed, we diagnose the fault and provide a separate repair quote before proceeding.

Do you repair all brands?

Yes. We service NordicTrack, Sole, Horizon, Precor, Life Fitness, ProForm, Bowflex, Matrix, Technogym and most other major brands.

How long does a tune-up take?

Most single-machine tune-ups take 45 to 90 minutes depending on condition and access. We confirm the expected window when you book.

Is the tune-up covered by a warranty?

Any parts we supply and labour we perform are covered by a 30-day parts and labour warranty. Wear items replaced at your request are noted on the invoice.
