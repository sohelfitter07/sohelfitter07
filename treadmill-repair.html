Common failures

Treadmill Symptoms We Fix

If your treadmill is unsafe, noisy or not responding, stop using it until a technician checks the machine. These are the issues we diagnose most often.

Treadmill won't start

A blown fuse, faulty power switch, damaged cord or failed control board can stop the machine from powering on. We test the full power path before replacing parts.

Belt slipping or off-centre

A loose or worn belt can slip during a workout and become a safety risk. We inspect belt tension, roller alignment and deck wear, then tension or replace as needed.

Grinding or squeaking

Worn motor brushes, bearings, rollers or a dry deck can create noise. We isolate the source and repair it rather than masking it with lubricant.

Console errors or blank display

Error codes, unresponsive buttons and blank screens often trace to the console, data cable or lower control board. We diagnose the electronics before recommending a replacement.

Incline not working

Incline motors, limit switches and controllers wear out over time. We repair the incline system and recalibrate it so the deck moves smoothly and safely.

Speed fluctuating or surging

Inconsistent speed can come from a worn drive belt, failing motor controller or speed sensor. We measure the drive system under load and correct the fault.
