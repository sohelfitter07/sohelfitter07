Why plans matter

Problems Preventative Maintenance Prevents

Unmaintained equipment fails at the worst possible time. These are the warning signs that a scheduled plan is overdue.

Unexpected downtime

A failed belt or controller can take a machine out of service for days without warning.

Rising repair costs

Small wear items become major repairs when lubrication, tension and calibration are ignored.

Repeat breakdowns

The same machine failing again usually means the underlying cause was never addressed.

Missed safety checks

Emergency stops, cables, guards and hardware need documented regular inspection.

No service records

Warranty claims, audits and resale values all depend on maintenance history.

Member complaints

Noisy, wobbly or inconsistent machines drive members away from a facility.

Questions & answers

Maintenance Plan FAQs

How the plans work, what they cover and how to choose one.

What is included in a maintenance visit?

A visit includes inspection, lubrication, belt and cable checks, calibration, hardware tightening, electronic diagnostics and a functional test. You receive a summary of findings and any recommended repairs.

How do I choose between Basic and Premium?

Basic suits small facilities with up to 15 machines and monthly servicing. Premium suits larger or high-traffic facilities that need weekly visits, unlimited coverage and 24/7 emergency priority.

Do plans include parts and repairs?

Plan members receive a discount on parts and repairs, but parts are not included unless stated in the contract. Any repair is quoted and approved before work begins.

Is there a contract term?

Plan terms are confirmed in the agreement. We offer flexible arrangements for seasonal facilities and multi-location operators. Ask for details during your assessment.

Do you maintain home gyms as well as commercial gyms?

Yes. The Home Gym Elite plan covers residential equipment, while the commercial plans are designed for gyms, condos, hotels and corporate fitness rooms.

Can you add machines to an existing plan?

Yes. Additional machines can usually be added at a prorated rate. We update the coverage and pricing after a quick equipment review.
