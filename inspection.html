What we look for

Inspection Issues We Flag

Most equipment failures leave warning signs. These are the problems our inspections are designed to catch before they become expensive.

Worn belts and decks

Glazing, fraying, loose tension and deck wear that reduce grip and stress the motor.

Motor and controller wear

Overheating, worn brushes, damaged capacitors and controller faults that cause shutdowns.

Frame and hardware issues

Loose bolts, cracked welds, worn bushings and stabilizers that affect safety and alignment.

Electrical and console faults

Damaged cords, poor grounding, error codes, blank displays and intermittent connections.

Safety hardware

Emergency-stop function, safety keys, guards, labels and locking mechanisms.

Lubrication and cleanliness

Dry decks, contaminated belts, blocked vents and build-up that shortens equipment life.

How it works

Our 5-Step Inspection Process

A consistent inspection method so nothing important gets missed.

1

Initial assessment

We review the facility, equipment list and any known issues before testing begins.

2

Functional testing

Each machine is operated through its full range to check performance, noise and response.

3

Component analysis

Motors, belts, bearings, cables, electronics and safety hardware are inspected closely.

4

Safety verification

Emergency stops, safety keys, guards, cords and structural integrity are checked.

5

Written report

You receive findings, photos where useful, prioritized recommendations and next steps.

Questions & answers

Equipment Inspection FAQs

What to expect before, during and after an inspection.

What does an inspection include?

We inspect the machine visually and functionally, test the drive and safety systems, check belts, cables, bearings, electronics and hardware, and provide a written summary with prioritized recommendations.

How long does an inspection take?

A single machine usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. A multi-machine facility depends on the equipment count and access; we confirm the expected window when you book.

Do I need to be present?

We recommend that you or an authorized adult be available to provide access, answer questions and receive the findings. For commercial sites, a facility contact should be on hand.

Will you repair issues found during the inspection?

Only if you approve. The inspection is independent of the repair. If you want us to proceed, we provide a written quote first and back the work with a 30-day parts and labour warranty.

Do you inspect commercial gyms and condos?

Yes. We inspect gyms, condos, hotels, corporate fitness rooms and home gyms. Multi-machine inspections can include a prioritized report for budgeting.

How often should equipment be inspected?

Most home equipment benefits from an annual inspection. Commercial facilities with heavy use often choose quarterly or monthly checks depending on machine count and traffic.
