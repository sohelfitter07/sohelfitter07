Questions & answers

Equipment Removal FAQs

What we take, how the estimate works and what happens to the equipment.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We provide a free estimate based on the equipment, quantity, location and access. There is no obligation to book, and the confirmed price is provided before the crew arrives.

What equipment do you remove?

We remove treadmills, ellipticals, exercise bikes, rowers, cable machines, weight benches, free weights, strength machines, home gyms and full commercial gyms.

Do you recycle the equipment?

Yes. We divert the majority of materials from landfill through certified recycling facilities, and over 90% of materials are typically recycled.

Can you remove equipment from a basement or upper floor?

Yes. Our crew is equipped to disassemble and carry equipment through stairs and tight doorways. Please mention access details when requesting the estimate.

How soon can you remove my equipment?

Same-week removal is available in most cases. If you have a move-out or renovation deadline, tell us when you call and we will confirm the earliest slot.

Are you insured?

Yes. Our removal crew is fully insured, and we take care to protect floors, walls and doorways during the removal.
