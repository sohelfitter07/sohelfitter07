Do you charge a travel fee?

No travel fee is charged within 20km of Hamilton. For locations beyond 20km and within our 120–140 km service area, any travel fee is quoted upfront before the visit is booked.

How fast can you get to Oakville?

Oakville is roughly 45–50km from Hamilton, so it usually falls into our next-day or scheduled service window. Same-day service is available when the schedule allows. Call us and we will confirm the earliest arrival time.

What areas do you cover?

We cover a 120–140 km radius around Hamilton, including the entire GTA, Hamilton, Ancaster, Stoney Creek, Burlington, Oakville, Mississauga, Brampton, Toronto, the Niagara Region, Brantford, Cambridge, Guelph and Kitchener-Waterloo.

Is same-day service available in my area?

Same-day service is available within 30km of Hamilton, subject to schedule availability. For locations beyond 30km, we usually offer next-day or scheduled appointments.

Do you service commercial gyms and homes?

Yes. We repair equipment in homes, condos, hotels, corporate fitness rooms and commercial gyms. Commercial clients can also request scheduled maintenance and priority response.
