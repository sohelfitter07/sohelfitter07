Common problems

Equipment Problems We Solve

From a treadmill that will not start to a cable machine with a frayed cable, these are the issues our technicians diagnose and repair most often.

Treadmill faults

Belt slipping, motor grinding, console errors, incline failure and machines that will not power on.

Elliptical & bike faults

Resistance problems, stride noise, pedal and crank wear, console faults and drive-system vibration.

Strength equipment faults

Frayed cables, worn pulleys, weight-stack issues, upholstery damage and adjustment mechanisms.

Electronic faults

Error codes, blank displays, control-board failures, sensor faults and communication problems.

Mechanical wear

Bearings, belts, chains, rollers and decks that are noisy, loose or past their service life.

Safety concerns

Loose frames, damaged power cords, missing safety keys and equipment that moves or wobbles.

Questions & answers

Fitness Equipment Service FAQs

Still unsure which service you need? Call us and describe the symptom — we'll point you in the right direction.

What services do you offer?

We repair and maintain treadmills, ellipticals, exercise bikes, rowers and strength equipment. We also provide inspections, preventative maintenance plans, PCB repair, installation, relocation and full equipment removal.

How much does a service call cost?

The on-site diagnostic is $89 and is credited toward your approved repair. Most common repairs fall between $150 and $400 depending on the parts required. You receive a written quote before work begins.

Do you service both homes and commercial gyms?

Yes. We work in home gyms, condos, hotels, corporate fitness rooms and commercial facilities. Commercial clients can choose scheduled maintenance and priority response.

How quickly can you attend?

Same-day and next-day appointments are usually available across Hamilton and the GTA. Emergency and after-hours service is available for commercial gyms and urgent residential repairs.

Do you repair all major brands?

Yes. We service NordicTrack, Sole, Horizon, Precor, Life Fitness, ProForm, Bowflex, Matrix, Technogym and most other major residential and commercial brands.

Are your repairs guaranteed?

Every repair includes a 30-day parts and labour warranty. If the same issue returns within that period, we return and make it right at no charge.
