Questions & answers

Installation & Relocation FAQs

What to expect before, during and after your installation or move.

How long does installation take?

Most single-machine installations take one to two hours. Multiple pieces or complex setups can take two to four hours. We confirm an expected window when you book.

Do I need to be present?

Yes. We recommend an authorized adult be available to approve placement, provide access and receive the operational walkthrough.

Do you assemble equipment straight from the box?

Yes. We unbox, assemble and set up new equipment, and we remove the packaging when we are finished.

Can you move equipment up or down stairs?

In most cases, yes. We assess access, stairs and doorway widths before the visit so the right crew and equipment are assigned.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Hamilton and the Greater Toronto Area, including Burlington, Oakville, Mississauga, Ancaster, Dundas, Stoney Creek and surrounding communities.

What if parts are missing or damaged?

We carry common hardware and can help source manufacturer parts. If a replacement part is required, we confirm the lead time and book a return visit.
