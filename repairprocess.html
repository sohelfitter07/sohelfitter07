Where repairs start

Problems That Bring Customers to Us

Most service calls begin with one of these symptoms. Diagnosing the cause early usually keeps the repair smaller and less expensive.

Machine will not start

Power, fuse, switch, cord or control-board faults can all stop a machine from turning on.

Grinding or squeaking

Worn bearings, motor brushes, rollers or a dry deck are common sources of noise.

Belt or cable problems

Slipping belts, frayed cables and worn pulleys affect safety and performance.

Error codes or blank display

Console, data cable and lower-board faults often show up as codes or dead screens.

Incline or resistance failure

Motors, sensors and limit switches can stop responding or move inconsistently.

Performance feels unstable

Speed surges, resistance drop-off and intermittent shutdowns point to drive-system faults.

Questions & answers

Repair Process FAQs

What happens after you call, and what you can expect at each stage.

How much does the diagnostic cost?

The on-site diagnostic is $89 and is credited toward your approved repair. You receive a written quote before any work begins.

Do you order parts before I approve the quote?

No. We diagnose first, explain the options and provide a written quote. Parts are ordered only after you approve the repair.

How long does a typical repair take?

Many common repairs are completed in one visit. If a special-order part is required, we confirm the lead time and book the return visit before leaving.

What happens if the repair does not fix the issue?

We test the machine under load before handover. If the same issue returns within 30 days, we come back and make it right at no charge.

Do you repair commercial gyms after hours?

Yes. Commercial clients can arrange evening, early-morning and emergency appointments to reduce disruption to members.

Can I get a quote before booking a visit?

We can give a preliminary range by phone, but a firm quote requires diagnosis. The $89 diagnostic is credited toward the repair.
